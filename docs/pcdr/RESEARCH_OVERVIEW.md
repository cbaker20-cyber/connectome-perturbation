## 1. Question and hypothesis

The question was whether a neuron set selected from a concentrated eigenvector of the signed connectome predicts a localized firing-rate response to removing its outgoing connections, beyond what connectivity and baseline participation explain. P means additional predictive value from eigenmode membership; D concerns degree/strength, C concentrated strong connections, and R recruitment under the input. These explanations can overlap. They are not four mutually exclusive outcomes.

[Pospisil et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07982-0) motivated structural mode selection and targeted perturbation. [Shiu et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07763-9) supplied the connectome-based spiking model. This study combines those ideas in a particular lesion experiment; it does not estimate an experimental effectome. Deleting outgoing connections changes the network operator and is not equivalent to stimulating along an eigenvector. Novelty has not been established by a comprehensive related-work review. See [original question and paper notes](PLAN.md#question).

The model uses materialization 630, 127400 modeled neurons and W[post,pre]. Twenty-one sugar-input cells are stimulated, not frozen or lesioned. Model-sign assignments, missing physiology and one anatomical reconstruction limit biological interpretation. An input seed is a simulation realization, not an animal.

## 2. Definitions that remain fixed

An outgoing lesion zeros a selected cell's outgoing weights. It does not clamp that cell's spikes or remove incoming connections. Baseline and lesion use the same scheduled physical input events. A separate record tracks actual delivered voltage jumps because delivery can depend on refractory state. Same seed alone was found insufficient to guarantee matching input.

For each neuron, first calculate lesion minus paired-baseline rate. Average the signed differences across seeds; then take absolute values. A is the sum of absolute mean changes inside the selected support divided by its cell count. F is that inside sum divided by the whole-network absolute sum; F is undefined when the total is zero. A has Hz units; F is a fraction. The code does not average each seed's absolute footprint as a substitute. See [readout implementation](../../eigencircuits/readouts.py) and [recorded protocol](PLAN.md).

The support is the smallest ordered set carrying 75% of squared complex eigenvector magnitude. That is a membership convention, not a prediction that 75% of the lesion response stays inside. Strong connections use a protocol-defined 5% incoming absolute-weight fraction; the exact percentage was not independently established as a universal physiological threshold. MN9 and motor rates are secondary model readouts, not observed feeding.

## 4. Decision rationale and what is not established

Pairing reduces variation caused by different input realizations, which makes a lesion comparison easier to interpret. It does not remove selection bias or establish generality across stimuli. The early schedule failure is why actual events are checked rather than trusting seeds.

The eigensolver checks distinguish numerical basis problems from candidate eligibility. The supplied toy's discarded imaginary component and unused seed were corrected. Near-degenerate modes require care because individual vectors can depend on basis choice. Passing numerical checks makes a reproducible structural candidate; it does not turn it into a dynamical circuit.

Matching addresses plausible alternative explanations. Degree/strength, strong-input structure, baseline rate, recruitment and motor composition were examined because they can affect lesion responses without special eigenmode organization. Mean standardized differences alone were insufficient: degree distributions still differed, and many comparison neurons overlapped. [Austin (2009)](https://doi.org/10.1002/sim.3697) motivates distribution checks as well as means, but supplies no fly-specific acceptance threshold. Eight eligible active excitatory motor alternatives are forced into every set satisfying the current strata. More such optimized sets would not create independent alternatives. The original 199-reference confirmation has not been completed.

The grid tests sensitivity to selected model parameters, not every physiological uncertainty. Input choices, one-second duration and initial 0.1 ms clock made the study tractable and consistent with the adopted workflow; they are not universally adequate settings. The later clock tests were necessary because exact replay only demonstrates reproduction at a fixed setting. The linear subthreshold solver still uses a clock for thresholds, synaptic arrivals and resets. See [Brian2 timing documentation](https://brian2.readthedocs.io/en/2.9.0/user/running.html).

Recruitment thresholds, clustering cut, 75% support, matching tolerances and grid points are operational research choices. Their exact values are not all derived from biology. Preserve their recorded rationale and test sensitivity where it matters; do not invent stronger justifications after seeing results. Specific concerns and papers are in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md), not retrospectively attributed to James or Dr. Muldoon.

The ten-cell recording was selected from early spike differences, explicitly after outcomes were known. It validated observation but missed the later recruited population. The 180 ms division and cell-specific pre-spike windows also use known response timing. They are useful for describing a failure condition; they are not held-out prediction tests. The 90.14% early-group positive-arrival fraction describes propagation within an outcome-defined group. Ten targets, including the earliest eight, have no such positive arrivals in their preceding 20 ms. No initiating neuron or pathway has been established.

## 5. Claims currently supported

For one selected structural support under sugar stimulation, default-setting average localization is reproducible across input seeds and close across the tested clock steps. It exceeds the fixed comparison sets on A and F in those default comparisons. Most absolute mean response remains outside the support, so this is relative concentration, not independence.

At the higher-weight setting, large population responses depend strongly and non-monotonically on time step. Their recorded events are reproducible, but convergence and mechanism remain unresolved. The recording and reconstruction checks establish what the model wrote, not that the physiology is accurate.

The main hypothesis is not confirmed beyond degree/strength, recruitment and composition. Confirmation would need a defensible comparison design and separately fixed evaluation; a claim across eigencircuits also needs more than this adaptively selected candidate. Additional seeds alone do not repair that limitation.
